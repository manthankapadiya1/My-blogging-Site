About this blog

PPSU Diaries is a student-run blog documenting everyday life at P P Savani University — the routines, the small chaos, and everything in between.

Why this blog exists

Most of what people hear about university life is either the highlight reel — fest photos, graduation day, placement announcements — or the complaints. This blog is an attempt to write down the ordinary middle: the 6 AM alarms, the lab sessions, the canteen conversations, and the hostel nights that make up most of an actual semester.

It started as a college assignment for a web development course, built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It's kept going because writing down a semester as it happens turned out to be worth doing anyway.

About P P Savani University

P P Savani University is a private university based in Kosamba, Surat, Gujarat, offering programs across engineering, management, pharmacy, design, and the sciences. This blog is written by a student on campus and reflects one personal perspective, not an official university publication.
